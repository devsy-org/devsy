---
title: What are Machines?
sidebar_label: What are Machines?
---

A machine is the VM a [machine provider](../managing-providers/what-are-providers.mdx) creates to host your workspace. Devsy creates and deletes a dedicated machine with its workspace. A shared machine can host several workspaces and remains until its last workspace is deleted.

To work with machines directly, use:

```sh
devsy machine
```
